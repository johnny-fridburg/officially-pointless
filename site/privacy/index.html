Privacy

You can browse Officially Pointless and personalize a certificate without signing in. We do not use marketing analytics, advertising cookies or a mailing list.

The certificate editor keeps its recipient and giver in your browser. Its downloads are generated locally, and those names are not sent with gift-kit checkout requests.

The seven public departments let you publish a display name, message and, for a museum exhibit, an optional photo. Your draft stays in your browser until you press the department’s publishing button. Published contributions are visible to other visitors through the department and its public link. Choose information and images you are comfortable making public.

Google sign-in is used to publish and manage public contributions. Google’s sign-in component loads when you open a public department with participation enabled. Your Google identity token is sent to our API to verify sign-in. We retain a pseudonymous identifier derived from your Google account ID to identify your contributions and limit repeated submissions. We do not save your Google password, identity token, account email or profile photo in the contribution store. The token stays in browser memory for the current page session; Google may use its own cookies and account settings, described in its privacy policy.

Museum photos are resized and converted to a bounded PNG before publication; embedded metadata is removed. Only that normalized image is stored with the exhibit. Original files stay on your device.

Public contributions remain until you or the operator remove them. Sign in with the same Google account and open the contribution’s link to remove it. Recent contribution records also support rate limits and safe retries; records older than 24 hours are cleared during the next publication. Hosting backups can retain removed content temporarily. For removal or privacy questions, contact support@officiallypointless.com with the public link.

Microsoft Azure hosts the site and stores public contributions in a private storage container. It processes technical request information to deliver and protect the service. Microsoft’s handling is described in its privacy statement. The public API exposes contribution content, not account identifiers or storage credentials.

When gift-kit checkout is available, Gumroad handles payment and private digital delivery. Our site sends only the fixed product code to request its approved checkout destination. We do not collect payment-card details on this site. Gumroad presents its own privacy information at checkout.

Johnny Fridburg operates this site. If you contact support, your email address and message are processed through Spaceship forwarding and our email provider to respond and manage related follow-up. Support correspondence is not added to a marketing list. Send only what is needed for your request.

The support contact on Google’s sign-in screen is a Google Group managed by the operator. Messages sent to that address are handled by Google Groups; its conversations and membership are private. The website’s main support address remains support@officiallypointless.com.

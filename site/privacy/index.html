Privacy

These free tools run in your browser. We do not offer accounts, marketing analytics, advertising cookies or a mailing list on this site.

Names, messages and selected images are used in your current browser tab to make the preview. They are not sent to our server by the tools. Downloads are generated locally. Your browser may retain downloaded files and browsing history under its own settings.

Microsoft Azure hosts this website. Like other hosting providers, it processes technical request information to deliver and protect the service. Microsoft's handling is described in its privacy statement. We have not enabled a separate marketing analytics service.

When gift-kit checkout is available, Gumroad handles payment and private digital delivery. Our site sends only the fixed product code to request its approved checkout destination; preview names, messages and images are not sent with that request. We do not collect payment-card details on this site. Gumroad presents its own privacy information at checkout.
